Property Feasibility 3D Model

Project Overview

  • Situation: Evaluated a potential property purchase lacking a floor plan.
  • Task: Determine the scale and feasibility of repairs before committing to a decision.
  • Action: Manually measured the site during visits and built a proportional 3D environment in Sweet Home 3D.
  • Result: Successfully visualized the spatial constraints to inform the repair viability.
